Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Atlas Uploader is a private tool run by its owner for their own use. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it uses Google Drive and Google Cloud Storage to upload, list and organise the owner's own video files, and to move duplicate copies to Drive's trash.

What it stores

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own server. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
